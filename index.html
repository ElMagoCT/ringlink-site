Features

Switch on. Join the Wi-Fi.
Race.

The first ring on makes an open Wi-Fi network, LED-Rings. Join it and the controls pop up at rings.local. Every other ring follows over ESP-NOW. No router, no app store.

22 effects, five that run down the course

Course Wave, Relay, Ripple, Alternate and Course Rainbow follow your gate order, so the light travels the track the way the pilot does.

Course Wave

Up to 50 rings

Each ring relays what it hears, up to seven hops, so gates about 50 ft apart stay in sync.

One 18650, USB-C

Battery % per ring in the app. A low ring blinks red, then turns its LEDs off before the cell runs flat.

Updates every ring

Upload one file and the host updates every ring it can reach, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

8 to 24 in

Five sizes, one tripod mount. Each ring measures its own LED strip the first time it runs.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need internet or a router?

No. The rings make their own Wi-Fi network and talk to each other over ESP-NOW. Your phone joins it and opens rings.local.

How many rings, how far apart?

Up to 50 rings in one set. Each ring passes messages on, up to seven hops, so gates about 50 ft apart work. We've flown four together so far.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. The control page works in Safari. For Bluetooth control on iPhone, use the free Bluefy browser.

Can I sell rings I build?

Not without asking. Everything is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: free to build, share and change for non-commercial use, with credit.
