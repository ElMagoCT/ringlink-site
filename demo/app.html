Radio (all rings)

Under each ring: signal from the host (dBm), the share of the host's messages it hears, and how many rings relay them. For gates ~50 ft apart aim for 90%+ and better than about -85 dBm. Rings out of the host's reach still work through relays. More power = more range, but some SuperMini boards get unstable above ~13 dBm.

The trace is the sensor of the ring you're connected to (the host), 1200 samples a second. Orange lines = detected passes; the band shows the threshold.

Detection (all rings)

Threshold = how far the light must dip or jump, in noise units. Hold-off = dead time after a pass. Longest = anything slower counts as a lighting change, not a drone.
